Where a best responder gains

The best-response gain in each seat splits exactly into what an equilibrium opponent wins without adapting (M3) plus what adapting adds at each of the opponent's decisions. A decision's contribution is how often it arises (with the opponent at equilibrium) times how much more the best response earns there than the equilibrium mix, with the best response played afterwards.

This section analyses the estimated strategy itself, so its totals are the plug-in values, before bias correction.

System prompt (rules given to the subject) 
You are playing Kuhn poker, a two-player card game.
Your goal is to maximise your expected winnings.

Rules:
- The deck has three cards: Jack, Queen, King (King is highest).
- Each player antes 1 chip and is dealt one private card. The third card is not used.
- Player 1 acts first and may CHECK or BET (1 chip).
- If Player 1 checks, Player 2 may CHECK (go to showdown) or BET (1 chip).
- A player facing a bet may FOLD (forfeit the pot) or CALL (match the bet and go to showdown).
- There is at most one bet per hand. At showdown the higher card wins the pot.

You will be told your seat, your card and the actions so far, then asked for your action.
