Replay: Always the most aggressive action reference

scripted reference · sampled · Kuhn poker

Step through a hand. At each of the subject's decisions you see the situation exactly as the subject was told it, how often it chose each action (bar) against the equilibrium (tick), and the action taken in this hand. At the opponent's decisions you see the equilibrium mix it played from, the best response against this subject, and what that best response gains.

No play-mode match was recorded for this run, so the numbered hands are simulated from the estimated strategy against the equilibrium. Each query to the subject is independent and context-free, so simulated hands follow the same distribution as live play, but they were not played live. “Biggest gain” hands are constructed: the chance outcomes and actions leading to the situation where switching to the best response gains most, then the best response and the subject's most likely actions.
