Where a best responder gains

The best-response gain in each seat splits exactly into what an equilibrium opponent wins without adapting (M3) plus what adapting adds at each of the opponent's decisions. A decision's contribution is how often it arises (with the opponent at equilibrium) times how much more the best response earns there than the equilibrium mix, with the best response played afterwards.

This section analyses the estimated strategy itself, so its totals are the plug-in values, before bias correction.
