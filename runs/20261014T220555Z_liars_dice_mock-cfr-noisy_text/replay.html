Step through a round. At each of the subject's decisions you see the situation exactly as the subject was told it, how often it chose each action (bar) against the equilibrium (tick), and the action taken in this round. At the opponent's decisions you see the equilibrium mix it played from, the best response against this subject, and what that best response gains.

Rounds marked “played” are the first of 20,000 rounds the subject played against the equilibrium opponent, with its raw replies. “Biggest gain” rounds are constructed: the chance outcomes and actions leading to the situation where switching to the best response gains most, then the best response and the subject's most likely actions.
